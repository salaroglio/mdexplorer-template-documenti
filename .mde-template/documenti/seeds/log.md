# Wiki log

## TL;DR

What happened to the wiki, one entry per operation, oldest first: the sources brought into the wiki, the pages fixed by
hand, the checks. Entries are added at the bottom and never rewritten.

- An entry starts with `## [YYYY-MM-DD] action | subject`; the actions are `source`, `page`, `check`.
- A source in `sources/` that the log does not name is a new source.
- The conventions are in the `documenti-wiki` skill.
