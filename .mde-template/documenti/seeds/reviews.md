# Reviews and checks

## TL;DR

Here stay the reviewer's reviews (`review-<date>.md`) and the librarian's reports (`check-<date>.md`): the history of
how the wiki was read again and kept in order.

- They are not deleted: they are the proof of who checked what, and when.
- The documentation owner's decisions are in the round log.
- The fixes are made in the pages of `wiki/`, not here.
