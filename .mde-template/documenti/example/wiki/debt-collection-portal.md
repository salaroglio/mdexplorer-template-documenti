# The debt collection portal

## TL;DR

The portal through which the debtors of [Nordica Crediti](nordica-crediti.md) see their positions and pay online. The
first version goes live on 15 September 2027, with PagoPA for payments and SPID for access.

- Go-live: 15 September 2027 (it was 30 June: moved by the email of 20/03).
- Payments with PagoPA; access with SPID, asked on 20/03.
- The positions' data arrive every night from the client's management system, in CSV.

## What it does

- It shows debtors their positions and lets them pay online (source: [minutes of 12/03](../sources/minutes-2027-03-12.md), § Topics).
- It pays through the PagoPA circuit (source: [minutes of 12/03](../sources/minutes-2027-03-12.md), § Decisions; confirmed by the [email of 20/03](../sources/email-2027-03-20-go-live.md)).
- The debtor logs in with SPID (source: [email of 20/03](../sources/email-2027-03-20-go-live.md)).
