# Minutes of the kick-off meeting — Nordica Crediti, 12 March 2027

Present: Giulia Ferri (Nordica Crediti, head of debt collection), Marco Neri (Pentagroup, project manager),
Sara Conti (Pentagroup, analyst).

## Topics

1. The debt collection portal: Nordica Crediti wants debtors to see their positions and pay online.
2. The volumes: about 40,000 open positions, with a peak in January.
3. The integration: the positions' data arrive every night from Nordica Crediti's management system, in a CSV file.

## Decisions

- The first version of the portal goes live by 30 June 2027.
- Online payment uses the PagoPA circuit.
- Giulia Ferri is Nordica Crediti's reference for the requirements.
