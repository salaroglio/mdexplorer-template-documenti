# The theory of Document Driven Development

## TL;DR

This folder explains the idea the common layer of MdExplorer projects stands on: written knowledge that grows over time,
kept in order by people and agents together. Six short chapters, to read in order or to have the MarkAgent explain.
- The heart is the wiki for LLMs: untouched sources, curated pages, written rules.
- Three operations keep it alive: adding a source, answering a question, checking its health.
- Three agents prepare the work, people approve.

## What it is

It is the theory of the method «Document Driven Development», the common layer that every composed MdExplorer
project has. It is not a manual of the program: it is the *why* and the *how* of a way of working.

You have it in the project because you asked for it when you created the project, or later, from Project settings →
«Download the theory». It is yours: you can annotate it, correct it, remove it. MdExplorer does not overwrite it.

## How to use it with the MarkAgent

The MarkAgent reads these documents the way you do. You can ask it, for example:

- «Explain chapter 2 of the theory to me and give me an example on our project.»
- «What is the difference between the log and the index? Answer in five lines.»
- «I put some minutes in the sources: let's do the ingest together, step by step.»
- «Run a health check of the wiki and tell me the three most urgent things.»

The MarkAgent proposes the changes and asks you before touching the files. If one of its answers does not match what
you read here, tell it: the written theory counts more than its memory.

## The words that come back

- **Source**: a document that comes from outside (minutes, a tender specification, an article). It is not modified.
- **Page**: a wiki document, written and kept up to date by the group.
- **Index**: the page you start from to find all the others.
- **Log**: the diary of what changed in the wiki, and when.
- **Ingest, query, lint**: the three operations, explained in chapter 3.
